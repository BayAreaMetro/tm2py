# Auto Ownership Model

!!! info "Model Purpose"
    The Transponder Ownership Model determines whether a household owns a toll transponder. 

## Model Overview

**Number of Models:** 1  
**Decision-Making Unit:** Household  
**Model Form:** Binomial Logit  
**Alternatives:** 2 (Yes or No)

### Purpose and Role
The Transponder Ownership Model predicts whether a household owns a transponder based on: 

- Percent of households in TAZ with income > $75k
- Percent of households in TAZ with multiple autos
- Number of autos owned by the household
- Expected travel time savings to and from work, minutes
- Straight line distance from transponder facilities, miles
- Percent increase in time to downtown avoiding transponder segments entirely
- Average transit accessibility to non-mandatory MAZs in TAZ
